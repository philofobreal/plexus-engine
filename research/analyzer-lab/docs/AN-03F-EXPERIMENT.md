# AN-03F: soft peaks and two-library reference acceptance

Owner: root analyzer/lab integration. Existing v1-v6 sources, old tests and measurements remain immutable. No runtime, worker, UI or default-algorithm activation changes. Third-party source/weights are not ported; independent Plexus DSP is evaluated against retained numerical evidence.

User-requested rule: only agreements of two different third-party libraries in the same declared capability/measurement become eligible development references. Select librosa 0.11.0 and audioFlux 0.1.9 for coarse acoustic onsets. They process identical channel-0 float32 PCM at original sample rate, no resampling. AudioFlux uses its native STFT, centered zero padding, HANN,2048 FFT,512 hop, linear-magnitude FLUX, explicit NoveltyParam [1,1,1,0,1,0,1,1], filter order 1. Librosa preserves its AN-03E onset_strength/onset_detect parameters (2048 FFT,512 hop,center=True,backtrack=False). Both package implementations involve spectral change; their errors can correlate. Different spectral representations and peak pickers are recorded, not silently forced to agree.

Use one-to-one class-matched maximum-cardinality pairing with fixed 25 ms primary tolerance, plus 2/10/50 ms sensitivity curves. Each accepted reference has both votes, their bounded time interval, disagreement and mean representative timestamp. Mean time is not a sample-precise annotation. Missing/failed provider means no accepted reference. Unmatched outputs are retained but unaccepted and are not negative truth. Plexus never votes on its own reference. Accepted means two-library agreement, with confidence null and groundTruthStatus false, never independent annotation. Unsupported instrument/beat/section capabilities are not inferred from onset agreement. Do not increase tolerance after examining music to manufacture agreement.

Recording development replays exactly the nine AN-03E excerpts: 20 s at 15/45/75% of each track,100 ms context each side,180 s exposure. Compare PCM and old librosa/Plexus timestamps to prior evidence. Original recordings stay local; runtime,wheels,PCM are ignored cache. Preserve program/binary/wheel hashes and all raw results/rejections. Annotated synthetic controls separately evaluate whether library consensus actually agrees with authored acoustic starts; they do not convert music predictions into ground truth.

DSP hypothesis: fourth-difference peak power can expose brief soft attacks diluted in the 4 ms mean. Add only sharp,raw-history-relative peaks with continued growth in second-difference power over 4 ms, initially ratio 1.3; preserve all v6 candidates with priority in 3 ms NMS. Source-removal/AM/beating/ramp negatives must not increase. Replay all 485 previously authored conditions before accepting any improvement; retain failed prototypes. Onset truth tolerance stays 2 ms, separately from the coarse oracle-pair 25 ms tolerance. Existing direct v5/v6 and production interfaces remain unchanged.

This initial peak/curvature design is a frequency-weighted waveform path, not yet a fixed-Hz multiband spectral detector. If gates fail, preserve the failure and narrow or redesign using physical evidence; do not lower a threshold until scores look better. Later transfer conditions must be declared before their outputs are viewed and not retuned within that frozen experiment.

Development 01 recovers 50 additional matches but adds six positive extras and one ramp false candidate, so it is rejected. Development 02 uses a 12 ms continuation-power window and requires a peak at least eight times the raw-relative floor; it recovers 11 additional D soft onsets without adding extras or negative candidates. Final code adds declared evidence metadata and rolling raw power with rebasing every 1024 proposal frames. Verify every candidate timestamp against revision 02 before accepting the optimization.

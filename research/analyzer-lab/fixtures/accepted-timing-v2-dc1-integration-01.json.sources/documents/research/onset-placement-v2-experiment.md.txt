# Conservative sharp-attack timing v2

Frozen before v2 measurement, 2026-10-14. Owner: Codex/root.
The retained v1 340-control result has 22 gradual-ramp refinements; all stay
historical evidence. This failure motivates development, so the same controls
are now regression/development, not unseen final tests.

Keep v1 executable unchanged behind explicit `local-band-v1`; v2 is a separately
identified `local-band-v2` path. Every v1 candidate/context/association gate stays.
After unique candidate selection, require complete original PCM over the next
12 ms. Mean raw power in the first 1 ms must reach at least 0.25 times mean power
over those 12 ms. This engineering sharpness gate deliberately abstains on slow
attacks; it is not probability, instrument identity or an audible-onset truth.
Preserve abstentions, counts and grid. No external label or musical-truth input.

Measure all previous controls without exclusions, v1/v2 positive matching and
negative refinements, protected timing strata and exact-recorded coverage/cost.
No threshold/range correction after inspecting results. Additional authored
rate/phase controls can test transfer but share a generator and are not independent
musical holdout. Default promotion still requires human-reviewed real music.
The initial 0.25/1 ms/12 ms selection is a design choice motivated by ramp onset
geometry, not a validated universal musical threshold.

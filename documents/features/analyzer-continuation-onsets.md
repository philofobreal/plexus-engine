# Experimental continuation onset filter

`extractContinuationOnsets(samples, sampleRate)` in `src/analyzer/ContinuationOnsets.ts` is an offline deterministic research entrypoint. It uses unchanged v5 proposals; it can remove candidates, never create new ones. The result has algorithm `experimental-continuation-v6` and a dependency-light type in `src/types/continuationOnsets.ts`. It is deliberately absent from TrackAnalysis, its JSON schema, analyzeAudio version selectors, browser workers and the general lab CLI. Version selector 6 still fails. The production default remains unchanged.

For a residual-v4-confirmed proposal, reuse the v5 raw 30 ms history and floor F=max(1e-20,rawPower*1e-5*(8000/fs)^2). Measure fourth-difference continuation power over 2 ms beginning five samples after its refined timestamp. Also compare mean second-difference power in the 12 ms preceding that timestamp and the 12 ms beginning five samples afterward. The normalized second-difference coefficients are [.25,-.5,.25]; sinusoidal amplitude response is sin(omega/2)^2. All power measurements have PCM amplitude squared units and are evidence, not calibrated probabilities.

Reject only when the residual-confirmation branch has full before/after directional windows, non-negligible raw history (>1e-18), fourth-difference continuation below F, and second-difference power falling. Do not reject an incomplete tail on this evidence. The fourth-difference direct branch is preserved. This conjunction empirically avoids the substantial soft-onset loss caused by the first experiment's continuation-only condition. It does not prove instrument removal: phase, cancellation, overlapping attacks and other recordings may defeat it.

Retained candidates keep original sampleIndex, proposalSample, time, evidenceSource, strength and innovationRatio. Their support extends through all directional reads; rejection records retain the same assessed support plus reason and the four powers used in the decision. Half-open support endpoints are clipped to real input length. Near endpoints retain endpointAmbiguous. The nominal lookahead bound is round(4 ms*fs)+5+round(12 ms*fs), never a measured realtime latency. Negative history indices use the inherited zero-boundary convention. Sorting and spacing remain the v5 subset order.

See [experiment declaration](../../research/analyzer-lab/docs/AN-03E-EXPERIMENT.md) and [validation](../../research/analyzer-lab/docs/AN-03E-VALIDATION.md). This bounded research improvement reduces removal counterexamples while leaving soft-onset recall unresolved. It must not automatically drive XR targets or visual accents.
